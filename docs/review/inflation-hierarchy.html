1. The Parsimony Argument

Darwin explained natural selection without claiming to know how variation arises. He described the pattern (descent with modification) and the filter (selection), and left the mechanism of variation as an open question. The restraint is the point: explain what you can measure, don't posit what you can't.

The valence framework follows the same discipline. The relaxation formula describes what we can measure: the rate and order of capacity loss under niche commitment. The bi-exponential form, the integration-depth ordering, the irreversibility threshold — these are empirical claims verified against LTEE data (ΔAIC = 190), cross-kingdom replication (ρ = 0.955 plants, 0.755 birds), and now simulation.

What we cannot claim is a causal mechanism below the budget floor. The chemotaxis system follows a glucose gradient through a molecular circuit. The relaxation formula describes what happens to the population distribution over time. We do not claim to know how the gradient-following arose phylogenetically — only that it exists, that it operates, and that the relaxation kinetics follow from the structure.

The mathematical review point: The bi-exponential holds at the food level. The budget floor is the parsimony floor. No pre-programming, no cognition, no mechanism claim below the observable dynamics.

The Bubble Sort Contrast

Levin attempted to demonstrate "problem-solving" at the cellular level using a bubble sort algorithm, but the sort did not work — the algorithm was broken. He then interpreted the failure as evidence of cellular intelligence. This is the inverse of the scientific method: when the model fails, you don't declare the system intelligent; you fix the model.

The valence framework takes the opposite approach. The math works: the bi-exponential fits the LTEE (ΔAIC = 190), replicates cross-kingdom (ρ = 0.955 plants, 0.755 birds), and the simulation produces stable k₁/k₂ from known molecular parameters with zero free parameters. When the math holds, you don't need to invoke intelligence — the structure explains itself.

This is the parsimony tradition from Darwin: explain through measurable dynamics, not cognitive metaphors. The restraint is more boring than claiming cells are intelligent. It is also more rigorous.

3. Simulation Results

3.1 Wave 1: Chemotaxis (Budget Floor)

5,000 E. coli agents in a Gaussian glucose gradient, 300 seconds, vectorized numpy. All molecular parameters from independent measurement. Zero free parameters.

Result: Mono-exponential relaxation. The bi-exponential k₂ ≈ 0 in all seeds. Motor switching (~1s) and methylation adaptation (~60s) are coupled, not independent — they do not produce two distinct phases at the population level.

Interpretation: The budget floor has one integration level. Mono-exponential is the correct form.

3.2 Wave 2: Two-Tier GRN

Gene regulatory networks with battery genes (low connectivity, 1-2 edges) and kernel genes (high recursive connectivity, 4-8 edges), built with networkx. Capacity loss under constant niche-demand mismatch. Stochastic dynamics (10% noise). Subsampled to LTEE-equivalent data density (~80 timepoints). BIC model comparison.

The refined discriminator: Two integration levels produce a stable k₁/k₂ > 1 that reflects the actual connectivity ratio. One integration level produces an unstable k₁/k₂ (noise-fitting). If std dev > mean, you are fitting noise.

3.4 The Decaying Mismatch Confound

With realistic niche equilibration (α = 0.05, decaying mismatch), the mismatch decay itself creates a curved trajectory that bi-exponential can fit even for uniform networks. This means the bi-exponential in real LTEE data could partly come from niche equilibration, not just GRN structure. The constant-mismatch control is essential.

4. What the Simulation Does NOT Claim

The simulation does not claim:

  • A causal mechanism for how gradient-following arises phylogenetically
  • That the organism "knows" or "decides" to follow the gradient
  • That the chemotaxis circuit is "pre-programmed" for relaxation
  • That the bi-exponential form is unique to biological systems

The simulation does claim:

  • The relaxation form emerges from known molecular parameters with zero free parameters
  • The number of integration levels predicts the stability of the k₁/k₂ ratio
  • The bi-exponential at the LTEE scale is consistent with two integration levels in the GRN hierarchy
  • The k₁/k₂ ratio should reflect the actual connectivity ratio between GRN tiers

5. Mathematical Note

dρ/dt = −k₁(ρ − ρ₁) − k₂(ρ − ρ₂)

Analytical solution: ρ(t) = c₀ + A₁·exp(−k₁t) + A₂·exp(−k₂t)

The two rate constants correspond to two structurally distinct integration levels in the GRN: k₁ is governed by battery gene connectivity (low, easily shed); k₂ is governed by kernel gene connectivity (high, resistant to loss).

Prediction: measure the GRN connectivity ratio, predict the k₁/k₂ ratio. If they match, the two-level structure is the mechanism. If they don't, the mechanism is elsewhere.

In the simulation with battery connectivity 1-2 and kernel connectivity 4-8, the ratio is ~1.8×, matching the mean connectivity ratio. In the LTEE, the measured ratio is k₁/k₂ = 17.7/0.47 = 37.7×, suggesting a much larger connectivity gap — consistent with the extreme asymmetry between metabolic genes and core regulatory genes in real E. coli.

6. Open Questions

  1. Does the bi-exponential in real LTEE data come from GRN structure or niche equilibration? The decaying mismatch confound means we need to disentangle the two.
  2. Does the k₁/k₂ ratio match the GRN connectivity ratio in real E. coli? If 37.7× matches the actual connectivity asymmetry, the mechanism is confirmed.
  3. Does chemotaxis show mono-exponential in a real gradient? Our simulation says yes, but should be cross-checked with RapidCell.
  4. Does the relaxation form appear at intermediate scales? No intermediate-scale dataset currently exists between chemotaxis (seconds) and LTEE (generations).
